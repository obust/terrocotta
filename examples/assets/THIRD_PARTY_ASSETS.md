# Screwbot third-party assets

The following Screwbot material textures are provided by
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) under the
[CC0 1.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Attribution is not required, but is included here to preserve provenance.

| Local file | Asset | Author | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `polyhaven-corrugated-iron-03-1k.png` | Corrugated Iron 03, 1K diffuse PNG | Charlotte Baglioni | https://polyhaven.com/a/corrugated_iron_03 |
| `polyhaven-hangar-floor-1k.png` | Hangar Concrete Floor, 1K diffuse PNG | Dimitrios Savva | https://polyhaven.com/a/hangar_concrete_floor |
| `polyhaven-cardboard-box-01-diffuse-1k.png` | Cardboard Box 01 model, 1K diffuse PNG | Rahul Chaudhary | https://polyhaven.com/a/cardboard_box_01 |

Screwbot samples a clean rectangular portion of the Cardboard Box model's
diffuse atlas at runtime, then draws its own projected seams, tape, and labels.
The original texture file is otherwise unmodified.
